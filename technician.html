⚡ MCB / RCCB Trip / एमसीबी बार-बार ट्रिप होना

MCB circuit ko overload ya short-circuit se bachata hai; RCCB earth-leakage detect karke shock risk kam karne mein madad karta hai.

Symptoms / लक्षण

Power achanak band hoti hai, breaker reset karne par phir trip hota hai, ya kisi appliance ko lagate hi RCCB girta hai.

Possible causes / संभावित कारण

Overloaded circuit, damaged appliance, moisture, insulation failure, short circuit, or earth leakage. Kabhi-kabhi breaker khud bhi worn ho sakta hai, lekin pehle asli fault dhoondhna zaroori hai.

Tools & materials / औज़ार और सामग्री

Flashlight, appliance labels/manual, notebook for noting symptoms. Electrical tester and insulation-testing instruments should be used by a qualified electrician only.

Step-by-step safe troubleshooting / सुरक्षित जाँच के चरण

1) Repeatedly reset na karein. 2) Agar safe ho to normal switches off karke plug-in appliances unplug karein. 3) Note karein kaunsa appliance ya circuit trip se linked hai. 4) Agar trip dobara aaye, panel kholne ke bajay electrician ko bulayein. 5) Electrician se load, insulation, leakage aur breaker rating verify karwayein.

Safety / सुरक्षा: Loose/overheated panel, burning smell, water ingress, repeated trips, or shock sensation means stop using the circuit and seek urgent professional help. Never bypass RCCB or fit a larger-rated MCB.

Approximate cost / अनुमानित खर्च

Repair cost fault, breaker type and rating par depend karta hai. Diagnosis aur parts/labour ka itemised quote lein; bina inspection fixed price dena reliable nahi.

Prevention / आगे से बचाव

Use correctly rated equipment, keep electrical areas dry, avoid overloading circuits, and arrange periodic inspection when equipment is used heavily. Keep the model/rating label and service records for future troubleshooting. / सही रेटिंग वाले उपकरण इस्तेमाल करें, बिजली वाले हिस्से सूखे रखें, सर्किट पर अधिक लोड न डालें और जरूरत के अनुसार जाँच करवाएँ।

Quick FAQ / सामान्य सवाल

Can I keep using it if the fault goes away? If there was heat, sparking, shock, burning smell or repeated tripping, no—get it checked first. For minor settings issues, follow the manufacturer manual. / अगर चिंगारी, जलने की गंध, झटका या बार-बार ट्रिप हुआ है, तो जाँच से पहले इस्तेमाल न करें।

💡 Light / Fan Not Working / लाइट या पंखा बंद

Light/fan issue bulb, supply, switch, regulator, capacitor or motor se ho sakta hai. Symptom ko dhyan se observe karna diagnosis mein madad karta hai.

Symptoms / लक्षण

Light flickers, bulb does not turn on, fan hums but does not rotate, speed is weak, or the device stops after heating.

Possible causes / संभावित कारण

Utility outage, failed bulb, faulty switch, loose connection, failed capacitor, seized bearing, low voltage, or motor winding fault. Same symptom ke multiple causes ho sakte hain.

Tools & materials / औज़ार और सामग्री

Correct replacement bulb and product manual for a simple lamp check; electrician may need suitable test instruments and rated replacement parts.

Step-by-step safe troubleshooting / सुरक्षित जाँच के चरण

1) Check whether other lights and appliances also have no power. 2) For a replaceable bulb, switch the lamp off and allow it to cool before replacing it with the correct type. 3) Check user-accessible fan controls only. 4) If fan hums, smells burnt, vibrates strongly or becomes hot, stop using it. 5) Ask an electrician to inspect switch, capacitor, bearings and motor as appropriate.

Safety / सुरक्षा: Do not open the regulator, ceiling rose, fan canopy or switchboard while connected to power. Isolate power and verify it is safe before any internal work; this should be handled by a qualified person.

Approximate cost / अनुमानित खर्च

Cost depends on bulb, capacitor, regulator, bearing or motor repair and access height. Ask for part and labour separately before approving work.

Prevention / आगे से बचाव

Use correctly rated equipment, keep electrical areas dry, avoid overloading circuits, and arrange periodic inspection when equipment is used heavily. Keep the model/rating label and service records for future troubleshooting. / सही रेटिंग वाले उपकरण इस्तेमाल करें, बिजली वाले हिस्से सूखे रखें, सर्किट पर अधिक लोड न डालें और जरूरत के अनुसार जाँच करवाएँ।

🔌 Switch / Socket Fault / स्विच या सॉकेट की समस्या

Switches and sockets wear with repeated use. Heat, discoloration or looseness can indicate poor contact and should not be ignored.

Symptoms / लक्षण

Socket has no power, plug falls out, switch crackles, faceplate is warm, or brown/black marks appear.

Possible causes / संभावित कारण

Worn contacts, loose terminal, damaged socket, overload, water ingress, poor plug fit or circuit fault.

Tools & materials / औज़ार और सामग्री

A flashlight and appliance manual can help with external observation. Socket tester and electrical tools are for a trained electrician.

Step-by-step safe troubleshooting / सुरक्षित जाँच के चरण

1) Stop using a loose, cracked, hot or discoloured outlet. 2) Unplug the appliance only if the plug and area are cool and safe to touch. 3) Check whether the appliance works at another known-safe outlet. 4) Do not dismantle the socket. 5) Have an electrician isolate the circuit, inspect wiring and replace damaged accessories with correctly rated parts.

Safety / सुरक्षा: Never insert a screwdriver or any object into a socket. Do not touch exposed conductors. If smoke, sparks or burning smell occur, keep away and isolate power only if the switch is safely accessible.

Approximate cost / अनुमानित खर्च

Cost varies with socket type, rating, wiring condition and labour. Replacement may be simple only after the circuit is safely isolated and checked.

Prevention / आगे से बचाव

Use correctly rated equipment, keep electrical areas dry, avoid overloading circuits, and arrange periodic inspection when equipment is used heavily. Keep the model/rating label and service records for future troubleshooting. / सही रेटिंग वाले उपकरण इस्तेमाल करें, बिजली वाले हिस्से सूखे रखें, सर्किट पर अधिक लोड न डालें और जरूरत के अनुसार जाँच करवाएँ।

🔥 Sparking / Heating / चिंगारी या गर्म होना

Sparks, crackling and abnormal heat can signal an unsafe electrical connection. Treat these as warning signs, not as a normal operating condition.

Symptoms / लक्षण

Visible sparks, burning smell, buzzing/crackling, melted plastic, a hot plug or repeated tripping.

Possible causes / संभावित कारण

Loose contact, damaged insulation, overload, unsuitable plug, internal appliance fault or moisture.

Tools & materials / औज़ार और सामग्री

No DIY repair tools are recommended while the fault is present. Keep people away and contact a qualified electrician; fire equipment must be suitable for electrical fires.

Step-by-step safe troubleshooting / सुरक्षित जाँच के चरण

1) Stop using the affected appliance/circuit. 2) Keep children and other people away. 3) If it is safe to reach the normal main switch without approaching smoke, heat or exposed wiring, switch off power. 4) Do not touch a hot or sparking plug. 5) Have the cause found and repaired before power is restored. 6) If a fire starts, evacuate and call emergency services.

Safety / सुरक्षा: Never throw water on electrical equipment. Do not keep switching the circuit on to “test” it. Do not use the appliance again until a qualified person has inspected it.

Approximate cost / अनुमानित खर्च

Because damage may be hidden inside wiring or equipment, get a diagnosis before estimating the cost. Ask for a written breakdown of parts and labour.

Prevention / आगे से बचाव

Use correctly rated equipment, keep electrical areas dry, avoid overloading circuits, and arrange periodic inspection when equipment is used heavily. Keep the model/rating label and service records for future troubleshooting. / सही रेटिंग वाले उपकरण इस्तेमाल करें, बिजली वाले हिस्से सूखे रखें, सर्किट पर अधिक लोड न डालें और जरूरत के अनुसार जाँच करवाएँ।

Quick FAQ / सामान्य सवाल

⚙️ Motor Starter / मोटर स्टार्टर

A starter provides controlled switching and overload protection for a motor. Pumps and three-phase motors need correct supply and protection settings.

Symptoms / लक्षण

Motor fails to start, starter trips, motor hums, pump runs weakly, or a three-phase motor sounds unusual.

Possible causes / संभावित कारण

Supply outage, low voltage, phase loss, overload setting issue, jammed pump, damaged cable, faulty contactor or motor winding fault.

Tools & materials / औज़ार और सामग्री

Record the starter make/model and indicator lights. Electrical measurements, panel tools and motor tests should be performed by a qualified electrician.

Step-by-step safe troubleshooting / सुरक्षित जाँच के चरण

1) Keep clear of the pump, belt and rotating parts. 2) Note the trip indicator or overload message without opening the panel. 3) Check externally for an obvious mechanical obstruction only when equipment is safely off and accessible. 4) Do not repeatedly reset the overload. 5) Ask an electrician to check supply/phase balance, motor current, overload setting, contactor and pump load.

Safety / सुरक्षा: Starter panels and three-phase systems can cause fatal shock or arc flash. Do not open, adjust or test live equipment. Never bypass overload or phase protection.

Approximate cost / अनुमानित खर्च

Costs depend on whether the issue is a setting, contactor, cable, pump jam or motor repair. Request diagnosis before replacing a starter or motor.

Prevention / आगे से बचाव

Use correctly rated equipment, keep electrical areas dry, avoid overloading circuits, and arrange periodic inspection when equipment is used heavily. Keep the model/rating label and service records for future troubleshooting. / सही रेटिंग वाले उपकरण इस्तेमाल करें, बिजली वाले हिस्से सूखे रखें, सर्किट पर अधिक लोड न डालें और जरूरत के अनुसार जाँच करवाएँ।

🔩 Contactor / Relay / कॉन्टैक्टर या रिले

Contactors and relays switch a load using a control signal. Their coil rating, contact rating and protection must match the equipment.

Symptoms / लक्षण

Contactor chatters, does not pull in, sticks, overheats, or the load does not respond to a control signal.

Possible causes / संभावित कारण

Incorrect/unstable coil supply, worn contacts, loose control connection, overload trip, wrong coil rating or failed control device.

Tools & materials / औज़ार और सामग्री

Useful information: model number, coil voltage label, control-system error and when the problem occurs. Internal tools and tests belong to a qualified electrician.

Step-by-step safe troubleshooting / सुरक्षित जाँच के चरण

1) Stop operating equipment if there is heat, smoke or unusual noise. 2) Note indicators and the sequence that causes the fault. 3) Do not push or hold the contactor manually. 4) A qualified electrician should safely isolate the panel, verify coil specification, inspect contacts, test control circuit and confirm overload/interlock function. 5) Replace only with a correctly rated component.

Safety / सुरक्षा: Never bypass an interlock, overload or safety control. Do not inspect an energised panel. A contactor that repeatedly chatters may be unsafe and should not be left running.

Approximate cost / अनुमानित खर्च

Cost depends on contactor size, coil voltage, brand and whether the fault is in the control circuit. Ask for the part rating and labour details.

Prevention / आगे से बचाव

Use correctly rated equipment, keep electrical areas dry, avoid overloading circuits, and arrange periodic inspection when equipment is used heavily. Keep the model/rating label and service records for future troubleshooting. / सही रेटिंग वाले उपकरण इस्तेमाल करें, बिजली वाले हिस्से सूखे रखें, सर्किट पर अधिक लोड न डालें और जरूरत के अनुसार जाँच करवाएँ।

🚰 Auto Water Level / ऑटो वाटर लेवल

Automatic water-level controllers use a float, probes or sensor to start and stop a pump. Correct cut-off prevents overflow and dry running.

Symptoms / लक्षण

Pump does not start at low level, does not stop at full tank, runs continuously, or shows an incorrect level.

Possible causes / संभावित कारण

Float stuck, sensor/probe dirty or damaged, controller failure, pump supply fault, cable damage or incorrect configuration.

Tools & materials / औज़ार और सामग्री

Check controller model and visible status lights. Do not use tools on wet equipment; electrical testing must be performed by a technician.

Step-by-step safe troubleshooting / सुरक्षित जाँच के चरण

1) Observe the tank and controller from a dry, safe location. 2) Look for a visibly obstructed float without touching electrical parts. 3) Keep water away from switches and control boxes. 4) If the pump will not stop or the tank is overflowing, stop it using a safe normal control if accessible. 5) Have a technician test sensor, controller, pump and independent high-level cut-off.

Safety / सुरक्षा: Never bypass the tank-full cut-off or work with wet hands/feet around electrical equipment. Do not enter a tank. If water reaches electrical gear, keep away and call a professional.

Approximate cost / अनुमानित खर्च

Cost depends on sensor/float type, controller model, cable condition and installation. Confirm the tank cut-off works after repair.

Prevention / आगे से बचाव

Use correctly rated equipment, keep electrical areas dry, avoid overloading circuits, and arrange periodic inspection when equipment is used heavily. Keep the model/rating label and service records for future troubleshooting. / सही रेटिंग वाले उपकरण इस्तेमाल करें, बिजली वाले हिस्से सूखे रखें, सर्किट पर अधिक लोड न डालें और जरूरत के अनुसार जाँच करवाएँ।

⏱️ Timer / Auto Switch / टाइमर सही समय पर काम नहीं करता

Timers automate switching by schedule. A correct clock, program, load rating and compatible contactor are important.

Symptoms / लक्षण

Equipment switches at the wrong time, does not start, stays on, or loses its schedule after a power cut.

Possible causes / संभावित कारण

Wrong time/day settings, incorrect program, power interruption, unsuitable load, faulty timer or contactor/control wiring issue.

Tools & materials / औज़ार और सामग्री

User manual, model number and a note of the intended schedule are useful. Use only the timer’s normal controls; wired-panel tools are for an electrician.

Step-by-step safe troubleshooting / सुरक्षित जाँच के चरण

1) For a plug-in timer, compare the current time and schedule with the manual. 2) Check AM/PM or 24-hour format, weekdays and manual/auto mode. 3) Note whether settings disappear after power loss. 4) Do not open a hard-wired timer. 5) Ask an electrician to check the supply, load rating, contactor and control circuit if settings are correct but operation remains faulty.

Safety / सुरक्षा: Do not use a small timer beyond its marked load rating or switch motor loads unless the manufacturer permits it. Never open wired equipment while energised.

Approximate cost / अनुमानित खर्च

Cost depends on timer type, switching load, enclosure and any contactor needed. Choose a rated device, not merely the cheapest unit.

Prevention / आगे से बचाव

Use correctly rated equipment, keep electrical areas dry, avoid overloading circuits, and arrange periodic inspection when equipment is used heavily. Keep the model/rating label and service records for future troubleshooting. / सही रेटिंग वाले उपकरण इस्तेमाल करें, बिजली वाले हिस्से सूखे रखें, सर्किट पर अधिक लोड न डालें और जरूरत के अनुसार जाँच करवाएँ।

👁️ Motion / Light Sensor / मोशन या लाइट सेंसर

PIR motion sensors detect changes in infrared energy; photocells respond to ambient light. Placement and settings affect performance.

Symptoms / लक्षण

Light stays on, fails to switch on, triggers too often or works only at certain times.

Possible causes / संभावित कारण

Blocked lens, sensitivity/time setting, daylight threshold, unsuitable mounting angle, heat/moving objects, wiring issue or sensor failure.

Tools & materials / औज़ार और सामग्री

Product manual and model number. A ladder or outdoor wiring work can add risk; use a qualified person when needed.

Step-by-step safe troubleshooting / सुरक्षित जाँच के चरण

1) Check the manual for the indicator and user-adjustable settings. 2) Remove visible obstructions from the sensor lens. 3) Check that the sensor is aimed at the intended area and is not facing a heat source, where applicable. 4) For a photocell, note whether it responds differently in daylight and darkness. 5) Have an electrician inspect mains wiring and sensor compatibility if settings do not solve it.

Safety / सुरक्षा: Do not work on outdoor wiring in rain, on a wet ladder or near wet surfaces. Never open a mains-powered sensor while energised.

Approximate cost / अनुमानित खर्च

Cost varies by sensor type, mounting height, outdoor rating and wiring requirements. Confirm detection area and IP rating before replacement.

Prevention / आगे से बचाव

Use correctly rated equipment, keep electrical areas dry, avoid overloading circuits, and arrange periodic inspection when equipment is used heavily. 📲 Smart Switch / स्मार्ट स्विच ऑफलाइन

Smart switches depend on power, Wi-Fi compatibility, app access and sometimes a cloud service. First separate network problems from electrical faults.

Symptoms / लक्षण

App shows offline, pairing fails, schedules do not run or the physical switch becomes unresponsive.

Possible causes / संभावित कारण

Router outage, unsupported Wi-Fi band, weak signal, app permissions, account issue, firmware problem or device power fault.

Tools & materials / औज़ार और सामग्री

Phone, router app, device manual and model number are enough for basic non-electrical checks.

Step-by-step safe troubleshooting / सुरक्षित जाँच के चरण

1) Check that the home internet and router are working. 2) Confirm the switch is supported by the correct app and Wi-Fi band. 3) Check app permissions, account and phone date/time. 4) Follow the manufacturer’s restart steps. 5) Write down automations before factory reset. 6) If the switch is physically hot, crackling or burnt-smelling, stop using it and call an electrician rather than troubleshooting online.

Safety / सुरक्षा: Never install a mains smart switch without suitable qualifications. Do not share Wi-Fi passwords publicly or install unknown apps/APKs to “fix” a device.

Approximate cost / अनुमानित खर्च

Cost depends on whether the fault is network configuration, device replacement or professional installation. Verify compatibility and warranty first.

Prevention / आगे से बचाव

🔀 Auto Changeover / ATS / ऑटो चेंजओवर

An automatic transfer switch changes supply between approved sources, such as mains and a backup generator. Interlocking prevents sources being connected incorrectly.

Symptoms / लक्षण

Backup does not take over, it fails to return to mains, indicators show an alarm or switching is delayed.

Possible causes / संभावित कारण

Source supply issue, generator/backup fault, control supply problem, sensor/setting issue, actuator fault or interlock fault.

Tools & materials / औज़ार और सामग्री

Record model, alarm lights and which supply was available. Internal inspection and electrical testing are for qualified technicians.

Step-by-step safe troubleshooting / सुरक्षित जाँच के चरण

1) Note the source status and alarm without opening the enclosure. 2) Check backup equipment using its approved normal controls. 3) Do not force a handle or reset repeatedly. 4) Contact a qualified electrician/ATS technician to test source sensing, transfer timing, interlocks and return-to-mains logic. 5) Confirm the system has been function-tested before relying on it.

Safety / सुरक्षा: Never connect mains and generator together or bypass an ATS interlock. Transfer equipment may contain multiple live sources even when one source is off.

Approximate cost / अनुमानित खर्च

Cost depends on ATS current rating, number of poles, control module and commissioning. A proper test after repair is important.

📟 Voltage Protection / वोल्टेज प्रोटेक्शन

Voltage protectors disconnect equipment when supply goes above or below a configured range. Repeated trips can indicate a supply problem that needs investigation.

Symptoms / लक्षण

Protector displays high/low voltage, cuts power repeatedly or reconnects frequently.

Possible causes / संभावित कारण

Utility fluctuation, overloaded supply, loose neutral, wiring fault, incorrect settings or protector failure.

Tools & materials / औज़ार और सामग्री

Record the display/error and time of occurrence. Do not open the protector or use exposed probes; measurements belong to a qualified electrician.

Step-by-step safe troubleshooting / सुरक्षित जाँच के चरण

1) Note the displayed voltage and whether several appliances are affected. 2) Unplug sensitive devices if it is safe to do so and follow their manuals. 3) Avoid repeatedly forcing reconnection. 4) Ask an electrician to investigate recurring fluctuations, neutral issues and circuit loading. 5) If neighbours also report a supply issue, contact the electricity provider.

Safety / सुरक्षा: Never bypass voltage protection or change internal calibration without the manufacturer’s procedure. A suspected loose neutral can damage appliances and create shock/fire hazards.

Approximate cost / अनुमानित खर्च

Cost varies by rating and protector type. If the supply itself is unstable, replacing the protector alone may not solve the underlying issue.

Prevention / आगे से बचाव

🔋 Inverter / UPS / इन्वर्टर या यूपीएस

Backup runtime depends on battery health, load, charging and ventilation. Alarms and error codes help narrow the issue.

Symptoms / लक्षण

Backup lasts only a short time, charging does not start, alarm sounds, unit shuts down or output is unstable.

Possible causes / संभावित कारण

Aged battery, excessive load, poor ventilation, charger fault, loose external connection or inverter fault.

Tools & materials / औज़ार और सामग्री

Read the model/error code and rated capacity. Battery testing and internal electrical work should be handled by a trained technician.

Step-by-step safe troubleshooting / सुरक्षित जाँच के चरण

1) Note the alarm code and whether the unit is on mains or battery. 2) Check the displayed load and switch off nonessential appliances using normal controls. 3) Keep vents clear and ensure the area is dry and ventilated. 4) Follow the manual for a normal restart. 5) Arrange a battery/charger test if runtime remains poor. 6) Ask for battery age, capacity and warranty before replacement.

Safety / सुरक्षा: Batteries can deliver very high current and may contain corrosive electrolyte. Do not open the casing, short terminals, mix incompatible batteries or work near flames.

🛡️ Earthing / Electrical Safety / अर्थिंग और सुरक्षा

Earthing and protective devices help reduce shock risk, but only when correctly installed and tested. A tingling appliance body is a serious warning.

Symptoms / लक्षण

Shock or tingling from metal casing, damaged earth pin, recurring RCCB trips or visible cable damage.

Possible causes / संभावित कारण

Broken protective conductor, wiring error, insulation failure, moisture or a faulty appliance. Never assume an earth connection is effective just because a wire is present.

Tools & materials / औज़ार और सामग्री

No DIY live test is appropriate. A qualified electrician may use approved instruments to test continuity, polarity, insulation and protective-device operation.

Step-by-step safe troubleshooting / सुरक्षित जाँच के चरण

1) Stop using the suspect appliance and keep people away. 2) If someone is being shocked, do not touch them while they may be in contact with electricity; isolate power safely and call emergency services. 3) Do not make an improvised earth connection. 4) Ask an electrician to test earthing and the complete circuit, not just visually inspect it. 5) Reuse the equipment only after the fault is corrected and tests pass.

Safety / सुरक्षा: Never connect earth to a random pipe, neutral or metal object. Do not touch damaged cables. Shock, smoke or burning smell requires urgent professional attention.

Approximate cost / अनुमानित खर्च

Cost depends on the fault and the scope of testing/repair. Ask for the test results and what was corrected, not just a claim that “earthing is okay”.

Prevention / आगे से बचाव

Use correctly rated equipment, keep electrical areas dry, avoid overloading circuits, and arrange periodic inspection when equipment is used heavily. 🔧 Fuse / Supply Fault / फ्यूज या सप्लाई की समस्या

A loss of power may be caused by a local circuit, appliance, building supply or utility outage. Identify the scope without opening electrical equipment.

Symptoms / लक्षण

One appliance, one room or the entire property has no power; a fuse or protective device fails repeatedly.

Possible causes / संभावित कारण

Utility outage, tripped protective device, damaged appliance, overloaded circuit, broken cable or service connection fault.

Tools & materials / औज़ार और सामग्री

A flashlight and notes about which rooms are affected are useful. Do not remove fuse carriers, meter covers or sealed utility equipment.

Step-by-step safe troubleshooting / सुरक्षित जाँच के चरण

1) Check whether nearby rooms or neighbours also have an outage. 2) Check only normal accessible switches and indicators. 3) Unplug affected appliances if safe. 4) If a protective device trips again, stop resetting it. 5) Contact an electrician for a local fault or the electricity provider for a suspected service/meter issue. 6) Keep away from fallen wires and damaged service cables.

Safety / सुरक्षा: Never bridge a fuse with wire or fit a higher-rated fuse. Do not touch meter/service wiring or tamper with sealed equipment. If a cable is down, stay far away and contact the utility/emergency service.

Approximate cost / अनुमानित खर्च

Cost depends on whether the issue is utility-side, circuit-side or appliance-side. Get a diagnosis before replacing parts.

Prevention / आगे से बचाव

Use correctly rated equipment, keep electrical areas dry, avoid overloading circuits, and arrange periodic inspection when equipment is used heavily. Keep the model/rating label and service records for future troubleshooting. / सही रेटिंग वाले उपकरण इस्तेमाल करें, बिजली वाले हिस्से सूखे रखें, सर्किट पर अधिक लोड न डालें और जरूरत के अनुसार जाँच करवाएँ।

❔ Other Electrical Issue / अन्य बिजली की समस्या

If the issue does not match another card, collect clear observations. Accurate information helps a technician prepare the right parts and avoid guesswork.

Symptoms / लक्षण

Intermittent power, unusual noise, unexplained tripping, equipment error or a problem that appears only at certain times.

Possible causes / संभावित कारण

Possible causes include supply quality, control settings, damaged accessories, overload, moisture or an internal equipment fault. The exact cause cannot be confirmed remotely from symptoms alone.

Tools & materials / औज़ार और सामग्री

Model/serial label, error code, when it happens and which equipment is affected. Photos should show external labels only; do not remove covers or touch wiring to take pictures.

Step-by-step safe troubleshooting / सुरक्षित जाँच के चरण

1) Stop using equipment that is hot, sparking, wet or smells burnt. 2) Note when the fault began and what changed beforehand. 3) Record whether one appliance, one circuit or the whole property is affected. 4) Write down error codes and recent power interruptions. 5) Give this information to a qualified electrician or authorised service centre. 6) Ask for the diagnosis and quote before approving replacements.

Safety / सुरक्षा: Do not try random wiring changes, bypass protection or follow unsafe online instructions. For shock risk, smoke, fire or water near electrical equipment, move away and seek urgent help.

Approximate cost / अनुमानित खर्च
