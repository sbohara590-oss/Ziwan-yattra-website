Battery Draining Fast

Battery percentage dropping quickly or unexpected shutdowns.

Open troubleshooting guide
  1. Open Settings and check battery usage by app.
  2. Reduce brightness and screen timeout.
  3. Restrict background activity for apps you rarely use.
  4. Update apps and system software when practical.
  5. If the phone is old or shuts down suddenly, ask a service centre to check battery health.

Stop using or charging a swollen or dangerously hot battery.

🌡️

Phone Heating

Phone becomes hot during use or charging.

Open troubleshooting guide
  1. Pause gaming, recording or other demanding tasks.
  2. Move the phone away from sunlight.
  3. Remove a thick case if safe and let the phone cool naturally.
  4. If it gets unusually hot while charging, unplug it.
  5. Check for system or app updates after it cools.

Never cool the phone in a refrigerator or freezer. Seek service if overheating continues.

🔌

Charging Problem

Slow charging or phone not charging.

Open troubleshooting guide
  1. Test a known-good compatible charger, cable and socket.
  2. Check for visible damage to the cable and plug.
  3. If a moisture warning appears, follow the manufacturer's instructions and let the port dry naturally.
  4. Do not insert metal objects into the charging port.
  5. If the issue continues, request a professional inspection.
🐢

Phone Lagging

Apps freeze, phone feels slow or screen stops responding.

Open troubleshooting guide
  1. Restart your phone.
  2. Check available storage and free space if nearly full.
  3. Update apps and remove unused apps.
  4. If one app is slow, clear its cache through Settings if available.
  5. Back up important data before considering a factory reset.
📦

Storage Full

Not enough space for photos, apps or updates.

Open troubleshooting guide
  1. Review large videos, downloads and duplicate files.
  2. Back up important photos before deleting anything.
  3. Remove apps you no longer use.
  4. Review messaging-app media folders.
  5. Empty the trash only after checking its contents.
🟩

Green, Pink or White Lines

Lines on the screen, flickering or display defects.

Open troubleshooting guide
  1. Restart the phone and see whether the lines remain.
  2. If possible, take a screenshot and view it on another device to help distinguish a display issue from a software issue.
  3. Back up your important data if the screen still works.
  4. Avoid pressing, bending or heating the display.
  5. Ask an authorised or qualified technician to inspect the display.

A software update cannot repair every physical display fault.

🛡️

Warranty Check

Find out how to check coverage for your phone.

Open troubleshooting guide
  1. Identify the phone model and purchase date.
  2. Find the manufacturer's official support website or app.
  3. Keep the invoice if available.
  4. Ask an authorised service centre about coverage, inspection and possible charges.

Warranty coverage varies by model, region, purchase date, damage and manufacturer policy. A green-line fault does not automatically guarantee a free repair.

🔐

Security & Suspicious Apps

Unfamiliar apps, pop-ups or suspicious account activity.

Open troubleshooting guide
  1. Uninstall apps you do not recognise or trust.
  2. Review app permissions and accessibility access.
  3. Update the operating system and use built-in security checks.
  4. Never share OTPs, passwords or banking PINs.
  5. If money is stolen or banking fraud is suspected in India, contact your bank and report it promptly through official cybercrime channels.
📶

Internet & App Problems

Wi-Fi, mobile data or apps not working properly.

Open troubleshooting guide
  1. Check whether the issue affects one app or every app.
  2. Toggle Airplane Mode off and on.
  3. Check mobile data, Wi-Fi connection and data allowance.
  4. Restart the phone and update the affected app.
  5. Check with your network provider if the problem continues.
